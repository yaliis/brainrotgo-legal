Privacy Policy

What this covers

Brainrot Go is a location-based game: it shows creatures near your real-world position on a map, and you walk to them and catch them. This policy explains what the game collects in order to do that, what it keeps, and who else sees it.

Brainrot Go is made by Shmock Studios. The game is in open testing — anyone can download it and play, and it changes often.

Location — reading it

While the app is open it reads your device's precise location, to place creatures near you, to draw the map around you, and to check that a catch happened where it says it did.

Location is foreground-only. The app never reads your position while it is closed or in the background, and it never asks for background-location permission.

Location — storing it

When you catch a creature, the game saves where and when you caught it: the coordinates, how accurate the GPS reading was at that moment, and the time. This is part of your save file. It is what lets the game show your catch history, award achievements based on how far apart your catches are, and notice impossible movement between two catches, which is how GPS faking is detected.

These catch locations are kept for as long as your account exists, and are deleted when it is.

Nothing else about your movement is recorded. Walking around without catching anything stores nothing at all, and your position is never shared with or visible to other players.

Drawing the map means requesting map tiles for the area you are looking at from Mapbox, our map provider. See "Who else handles your data".

Your account

Every player gets an anonymous account automatically on first launch. No sign-up, no email, no name. You can play the entire single-player game this way and never tell us anything about yourself.

If you want your progress to survive a lost or replaced phone, you can link an email address and a password. We store that email address and use it to sign you in and to let you recover access if you forget your password.

You can also link a Google account, or on iPhone an Apple account, instead of an email and password. Google or Apple then gives us your email address and a fixed account identifier, and nothing else: no contacts, no photos, no other data. We use them only to sign you in. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the private relay address Apple gives us.

If you set a display name, it is checked against a blocklist for unsafe, impersonating and contact-sharing names before it is accepted. Names that pass are visible to players you have added as friends.

When you accept this policy and the Terms, we record which version you accepted and when — both on your account and on the device — so the game knows whether it still needs to show you an updated version later.

Your age

When you create an account, the game asks for your date of birth. The date itself never leaves your phone — it is stored locally on the device, and only the resulting bracket (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) is saved to your account. See "Children and supervised accounts" below.

Gameplay data

We store what you would expect a save file to hold: the creatures you have caught and when, your coins and items, your level and experience, achievements, skins, friend connections, gifts, trades, Market activity, and when you last opened the game.

Your save lives on our servers so that it follows you between devices. A copy is cached on your phone so the game starts quickly.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads — the kind you choose to watch in exchange for something in the game. There are no banner ads, no ads between screens, and no ads when you open the app. You never have to watch one, and everything obtainable by watching an ad is also obtainable by playing.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Like every mobile ad network, AdMob receives a device advertising identifier and standard technical information — device type, operating system version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address — when an ad is requested. We also send AdMob your account's identifier, so the reward for a finished video is credited to the right account.

Ads are requested as non-personalised for every player, at every age. We do not ask AdMob to build a profile of you or to target ads based on your behaviour, and we do not send AdMob your location, your email address, or anything about what you do in the game. Ad content is capped at a teen-appropriate rating.

Because nothing here tracks you across other apps or websites, the app does not show Apple's tracking-permission prompt. There would be nothing for it to ask permission for.

During testing you may be shown Google's test ads rather than real ones. The data the ad software handles is the same either way.

Crash reports

When the app crashes we may collect a crash report — device model, operating system version, app version, and where in the code the crash happened — through Sentry, so the bug can be found and fixed.

Crash reports carry no location data, and we deliberately do not use Sentry's session-replay or performance-tracing features. Crash reporting is configured per build and is switched off unless a build carries a Sentry key.

What we do not collect

These are all things a game like this could do, and this one does not. They are worth stating plainly.

No analytics. No analytics service is connected to Brainrot Go, and no record of how you play is sent anywhere.

No push notifications, and no notification tokens. The app does not ask for notification permission.

No advertising profile, no cross-app tracking, and no data brokers. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

No microphone, no camera, no contacts, and no photo access. The app requests none of those permissions.

No background location.

Who else handles your data

A short list of providers process data on our behalf, strictly in order to run the game.

Supabase — our backend and database provider. Your account, your save file and your catch history are stored on Supabase infrastructure.

Google AdMob — rewarded video ads. Receives what is described under "Advertising".

Mapbox — map tiles. Receives requests for the part of the map you are looking at, which necessarily reflects roughly where you are.

Sentry — crash reports, when enabled. Receives what is described under "Crash reports".

Expo — hosts the app's over-the-air updates. When the app checks for an update it sends its platform and version.

This means your data is stored and processed on infrastructure that may be located outside the country you live in.

What other players can see

Nothing at all, unless you add them as a friend. Friends are added by exchanging an eight-character friend code. There is no name search, no public directory, and no way to find players near you.

A friend can see: your display name and friend code, roughly when you were last online (shown as "today" or "this week", never an exact time), your rank and weekly catch count on the friends leaderboard, and, during a trade, what you have put on the table.

A friend cannot see: your location, your email address, your age, your coin balance, or your collection.

The Market is anonymous. Listings and bids never show who posted them, to anyone.

One exception is worth naming. If you trade a creature to another player, their copy of that creature keeps a record of your display name as it was at the time, so they can see where it came from. That snapshot stays with their creature even if you later change your name or delete your account.

There is no chat or messaging in Brainrot Go, of any kind.

How long we keep it

Accounts linked to an email address are kept until you delete them.

Anonymous accounts are deleted automatically: after 30 days if the account never caught anything, and otherwise after 180 days without opening the game. Their save data goes with them.

An uninstalled anonymous account has no way to be identified or recovered, which is why linking an email address is the only way to keep progress safe.

Catch locations are kept for the life of the account, as described under "Location — storing it".

Deleting your account

If you have linked an email address, the app's Account screen has a Delete account button. It permanently erases your account and everything attached to it — creatures, coins, items, friends, achievements, and your catch history including its locations — from our servers. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you are playing as a guest there is no delete button, because there is nothing tied to you to delete. Uninstalling the app ends it, and the account is purged automatically on the schedule above.

Two things survive by design: a creature you traded away keeps your old display name on the recipient's copy, as described above, and completed Market transactions are kept with the identifiers removed.

Your rights, and how to use them

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, in the app, as described above.

For anything else — a copy of your data, a correction, or a question about how it is handled — email gckgamedev@gmail.com. There is no automated export tool, so these requests are handled by hand; please allow a little time.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your data. We will honour them, and the address above is the way to ask.

Children and supervised accounts

Brainrot Go is rated 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. When you create an account, the game asks for your date of birth first.

If you answer that you are under 13 you cannot create an account for yourself. You can keep playing as a guest — the map, catching and your collection all still work — or hand the phone to a parent.

A parent or guardian can create a supervised account for a child under 13. That flow shows the parent what is collected, uses the parent's own email address and password, and requires them to confirm that they are the parent or guardian. On a supervised account, friends, gifts, trading, the Market, the leaderboard, display names and any purchases are switched off. A parent can end it at any time by deleting the account, which erases everything.

We should be honest about the limits of this. It is consent asserted by an adult, from an email address they control, having read a disclosure. It is not the stricter "verifiable parental consent" that some laws define, which requires something stronger such as an identity or payment check. If you are a parent and want your child's data removed, email us and we will delete it.

Because anyone can play as a guest without ever answering the age question, we cannot always know a player's age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data travels to our servers over encrypted connections. Access to your save file is enforced by the database itself, account by account, so one player's data cannot be read by another. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. Administrative keys are never included in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and this one is being actively developed. Please use a password that you do not use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way we will update the date and version shown with it, and the app will ask you to accept the new version. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access, correct or delete your data: gckgamedev@gmail.com

Language versions

This document is available in English and in Hebrew. If the two versions differ, the English version is the reference text. That does not remove any right you have under the consumer-protection law of the country you live in.

מיקום — הקריאה שלו

כל עוד האפליקציה פתוחה, היא קוראת את המיקום המדויק של המכשיר שלכם — כדי למקם יצורים בקרבתכם, לצייר את המפה סביבכם, ולוודא שתפיסה אכן קרתה במקום שבו היא מדווחת.

המיקום נקרא רק כשהאפליקציה פתוחה. האפליקציה אף פעם לא קוראת את המיקום שלכם כשהיא סגורה או פועלת ברקע, והיא לא מבקשת הרשאת מיקום ברקע.

מיקום — השמירה שלו

כשאתם תופסים יצור, המשחק שומר איפה ומתי תפסתם אותו: הקואורדינטות, כמה מדויקת הייתה קריאת ה־GPS באותו רגע, והשעה. זה חלק מקובץ השמירה שלכם. זה מה שמאפשר למשחק להציג את היסטוריית התפיסות שלכם, להעניק הישגים שמבוססים על המרחק בין תפיסות, ולזהות תנועה בלתי אפשרית בין שתי תפיסות — כך מזוהה זיוף מיקום.

מיקומי התפיסה נשמרים כל עוד החשבון שלכם קיים, ונמחקים יחד איתו.

שום דבר אחר בתנועה שלכם לא נרשם. הליכה בלי לתפוס כלום לא שומרת דבר, והמיקום שלכם לעולם אינו משותף עם שחקנים אחרים ואינו גלוי להם.

פרסומות

המשחק מציג פרסומות וידאו מתוגמלות — כאלה שאתם בוחרים לצפות בהן בתמורה למשהו במשחק. אין באנרים, אין פרסומות בין מסכים, ואין פרסומת בפתיחת האפליקציה. אף פעם לא חייבים לצפות באחת, וכל מה שאפשר לקבל בעזרת צפייה בפרסומת אפשר לקבל גם פשוט על ידי משחק.

הפרסומות מוגשות על ידי Google AdMob. כמו כל רשת פרסום בנייד, AdMob מקבלת מזהה פרסום של המכשיר ומידע טכני סטנדרטי — סוג המכשיר, גרסת מערכת ההפעלה, ואזור משוער שנגזר מכתובת ה־IP שלכם — בכל בקשה לפרסומת. אנחנו גם שולחים ל־AdMob את המזהה של החשבון שלכם, כדי שהתגמול על סרטון שהסתיים ייזקף לחשבון הנכון.

הפרסומות מבוקשות כלא־מותאמות אישית עבור כל שחקן, בכל גיל. אנחנו לא מבקשים מ־AdMob לבנות עליכם פרופיל או לכוון פרסומות לפי ההתנהגות שלכם, ואנחנו לא שולחים ל־AdMob את המיקום שלכם, את האימייל שלכם, או משהו על מה שאתם עושים במשחק. תוכן הפרסומות מוגבל לדירוג שמתאים לבני נוער.

מכיוון ששום דבר כאן לא עוקב אחריכם באפליקציות או באתרים אחרים, האפליקציה לא מציגה את בקשת ההרשאה למעקב של Apple. פשוט אין על מה לבקש רשות.

בתקופת הבדיקות ייתכן שתוצגנה לכם פרסומות בדיקה של Google במקום פרסומות אמיתיות. הנתונים שתוכנת הפרסום מטפלת בהם זהים בשני המקרים.

דוחות קריסה

כשהאפליקציה קורסת ייתכן שנאסוף דוח קריסה — דגם המכשיר, גרסת מערכת ההפעלה, גרסת האפליקציה, והמקום בקוד שבו הקריסה קרתה — באמצעות Sentry, כדי שאפשר יהיה למצוא ולתקן את התקלה.

דוחות קריסה אינם כוללים נתוני מיקום, ואנחנו בכוונה לא משתמשים ביכולות הקלטת המסך או ניטור הביצועים של Sentry. דיווח קריסות מוגדר לכל גרסת בנייה בנפרד, והוא כבוי אלא אם הגרסה נושאת מפתח Sentry.

מה שחקנים אחרים רואים

שום דבר, אלא אם הוספתם אותם כחברים. חברים מתווספים על ידי החלפת קוד חבר בן שמונה תווים. אין חיפוש לפי שם, אין ספריית שחקנים ציבורית, ואין שום דרך למצוא שחקנים בקרבתכם.

חבר יכול לראות: את שם התצוגה וקוד החבר שלכם, בערך מתי הייתם מחוברים לאחרונה (מוצג כ"היום" או "השבוע", אף פעם לא שעה מדויקת), את הדירוג ומספר התפיסות השבועי שלכם בטבלת החברים, ובמהלך החלפה — מה הצעתם.

השוק אנונימי. הצעות מכירה והצעות מחיר לעולם לא מראות מי פרסם אותן, לאף אחד.
